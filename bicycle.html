Why Bicycle Safety Matters

Cycling is healthy, eco-friendly, and fun. By following simple safety rules, you can protect yourself and others on the road.

🪖

Wear a Helmet

Always wear a properly fitted helmet.

🚦

Obey Traffic Signals

Stop at red lights and follow all road signs.

💡

Use Lights

Switch on front and rear lights when riding at night.

👀

Stay Alert

Watch for vehicles, pedestrians and road hazards.

📱

No Mobile Phone

Never use a mobile phone while riding.

🦺

Wear Bright Clothes

Bright or reflective clothing makes you more visible.

Before You Ride

  • ✅ Check the brakes.
  • ✅ Check tyre pressure.
  • ✅ Tighten the chain if needed.
  • ✅ Ring the bell before overtaking.
  • ✅ Carry a water bottle for long rides.

Remember

A safe cyclist always respects traffic rules, avoids risky behaviour, and helps keep the roads safe for everyone.
