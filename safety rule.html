🚦 Road Safety Rules

Follow the Rules, Save Lives

🪖 Wear a Helmet

Always wear a properly fitted helmet while riding a two-wheeler.

💺 Wear a Seat Belt

Fasten your seat belt before starting a journey, even for short distances.

📱 Don't Use Mobile While Driving

Keep your full attention on the road. Avoid texting or calling while driving.

🚸 Use Zebra Crossing

Pedestrians should cross only at zebra crossings after checking both sides.

🚦 Obey Traffic Signals

Follow red, yellow and green signals without breaking traffic rules.

⚡ Follow Speed Limits

Drive within the speed limit to avoid accidents and ensure everyone's safety.

🚫 Never Drink and Drive

Driving after consuming alcohol or drugs is dangerous and illegal.

👶 Protect Children

Children should always be supervised while crossing roads and travelling.

🚴 Ride Safely

Use reflectors, lights and helmets while riding a bicycle.

🤝 Respect Other Road Users

Drive patiently, give way to emergency vehicles and respect pedestrians.
