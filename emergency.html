🩹 First Aid Tips

✔ Stay calm and ensure the area is safe.
✔ Call the emergency services immediately.
✔ Do not move an injured person unless there is immediate danger.
✔ Control bleeding using a clean cloth.
✔ Keep the injured person warm and reassure them until help arrives.

🚦 Accident Safety

  • Switch on hazard lights.
  • Warn other road users.
  • Do not crowd around the victim.
  • Wait for trained medical personnel.
  • Share accurate location with emergency services.
