Parents Play a Key Role in Road Safety

Home Parent Corner Rules Contact

🏠 Safety Starts at Home

  • Teach children basic traffic rules.
  • Always hold your child's hand while crossing roads.
  • Explain the meaning of traffic lights and road signs.
  • Set a good example by following traffic rules yourself.

🚌 School Travel Safety

  • Reach the bus stop early.
  • Wait in a safe place away from traffic.
  • Never run behind a moving school bus.
  • Teach children to board and exit the bus carefully.

🚲 Bicycle Safety

  • Ensure children wear helmets.
  • Check brakes and tyres regularly.
  • Use reflectors and lights at night.
  • Ride only in safe areas.

📱 Digital Safety

  • Do not allow children to use mobile phones while crossing roads.
  • Teach them to stay alert near traffic.
  • Avoid distractions while walking or cycling.
