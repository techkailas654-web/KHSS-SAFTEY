Safety Tips

  • 🚌 Reach the bus stop at least 5 minutes early.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Wait in a safe place away from the road.
  • 🚶 Stand in a queue while boarding.
  • 🤝 Hold the handrail when getting in or out.
  • 💺 Stay seated while the bus is moving.
  • 🙅 Never put your hands or head outside the window.
  • 🎒 Keep your school bag safely beside you.
  • 🚫 Never distract the driver.
  • 🚶 Cross the road only after the bus leaves and you can clearly see both sides.
⏰

Be On Time

Reach the bus stop early to avoid rushing.

🪑

Sit Properly

Remain seated until the bus completely stops.

🛑

Follow Instructions

Listen to the driver and bus attendant.

🚸

Cross Safely

Always use a safe crossing point after getting off the bus.

Remember

School bus safety is everyone's responsibility. Safe behaviour protects you, your friends, and the driver. Let's make every school journey safe and enjoyable!
